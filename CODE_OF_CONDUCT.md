# Community Code of Conduct

FreeRDC contributors and community members are expected to make participation
safe, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when giving technical feedback.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
  viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, security boundaries, and confidential reports.
- Accept correction, de-escalate conflict, and take responsibility for harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, impersonation, doxxing, publishing private information, or
pressuring anyone to reveal credentials or security-sensitive data are not
acceptable. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, reviews, release
spaces, and public events where someone represents the project. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when reasonably
necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
available private GitHub contact channel. If no private channel is available,
contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile to request one; do not post
sensitive evidence publicly. Reports will be handled as discreetly as
practicable, but confidentiality or a specific outcome cannot be guaranteed.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, patterns of behavior,
and the safety of affected people. Good-faith disagreement with a technical or
moderation decision is not itself misconduct.
